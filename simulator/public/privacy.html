Macro Coach Privacy Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026.

Macro Coach is a hackathon project. It stores only what you tell it: the foods you log, your nutrition goals and, if you provide them, your weight, height, age, sex and activity level. This data is kept in an Amazon DynamoDB table in the us-east-1 region and is used only to compute your nutrition summaries and suggestions.

Meal descriptions are sent to Amazon Bedrock to extract food names and amounts, and food names are sent to the USDA FoodData Central API to look up nutrition facts. No data is sold or shared for advertising.

The shared demo account is reset every night. To delete your data, open an issue at github.com/Wilhelmvsop/Macro-Coach.

Macro Coach is not a medical service. Nutrition numbers are estimates.
